# Top Gun: Maverick (2022)

> _“It’s not the plane, it’s the pilot.”_ — Captain Pete "Maverick" Mitchell

## Overview

**Top Gun: Maverick** is a 2022 American action drama film and the long-awaited sequel to the 1986 classic _Top Gun_. Directed by **Joseph Kosinski**, the film brings back **Tom Cruise** as the iconic Navy pilot **Pete "Maverick" Mitchell**.

---

## Key Details

- **Director**: Joseph Kosinski  
- **Writers**: Ehren Kruger, Eric Warren Singer, Christopher McQuarrie  
- **Starring**:
  - Tom Cruise
  - Miles Teller
  - Jennifer Connelly
  - Jon Hamm
  - Glen Powell
  - Ed Harris

---

## Plot Summary

After more than 30 years of service as one of the Navy's top aviators, **Maverick** is still pushing the envelope as a test pilot. When he's called back to train a new generation of **Top Gun** graduates—including the son of his late best friend, **Goose**—Maverick must confront his past while preparing the team for a dangerous mission.

---

## Themes

- **Redemption**
- **Mentorship**
- **Courage and Sacrifice**
- **Legacy**

---

## What Makes It Great?

- **Incredible Aerial Stunts**: Real flying, real G-forces
- **Emotional Depth**: Maverick’s arc feels both nostalgic and fresh
- **Strong Performances**: Especially from Cruise and Miles Teller
- **Epic Soundtrack**: Featuring _"Hold My Hand"_ by Lady Gaga

---

## Trivia

- All actors underwent intense flight training to perform in real fighter jets  
- The film was nominated for **6 Academy Awards** and won **Best Sound**  
- It grossed **over $1.4 billion** worldwide!
